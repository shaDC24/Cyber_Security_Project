\caption{Patient flow. Three queues compete for one shift-varying physician
pool, and the scheduling policy decides who is seen next. A patient sent for
diagnostics enters the testing subsystem, where each of the four stations
holds its own queue and serves one patient at a time. Before every remaining
test the patient re-checks the stations still on their list $\mathcal{R}_i(t)$
and joins the one with the smallest $n_j(t)\,\tau_j$, where $n_j(t)$ counts
the patient in service plus those waiting. Under the fixed-order rule the same
box is instead a sequence settled once, descending $\delta_j$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:rules}). The patient returns to the physicians through the
follow-up queue once the last ordered result has reported. Blue marks the
three decision points.}
\label{fig:flow}
